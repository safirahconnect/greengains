Who runs the GreenGains?

Thieron Stark is a 35-year-old personal trainer based in Burnley, dedicated to helping individuals achieve their fitness goals while promoting a sustainable lifestyle.

With extensive experience and a passion for health and wellness, Thieron offers personalized exercise routines, nutritional guidance, and eco-conscious fitness solutions either through his personal training or through his community.

He provides private lessons, combining his expertise in fitness, nutrition, and sustainability to support clients in leading healthier, greener lives.
