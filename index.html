Who runs the GreenGains?

Thieron Stark is a 35-year-old personal trainer based in Burnley, dedicated to helping individuals achieve their fitness goals while promoting a sustainable lifestyle .

With extensive experience and a passion for health and wellness, Thieron offers personalized exercise routines, nutritional guidance, and eco-conscious fitness solutions either through his personal training or through his community.

He provides private lessons, combining his expertise in fitness, nutrition, and sustainability to support clients in leading healthier, greener lives.

Become a GreenGainer today !

“Become what? What are you even on?”

Easy...let us explain. The GreenGainer community is our sustainable-driven fitness hub , that offers exciting events, challenges and customized packages. Once you complete the challenges and/or attend the events you’ll earn rewards.

Once you subscribe to one of our services you will receive an email with a link to join our private WhatsApp group . There you can connect and engage with others greengainers while enjoying:

  • Workshops, Retreats & Outdoor Trainings: Learn, grow, and stay active in eco-friendly ways

  • Online Meetings: Engage with the community and share your progress.

  • Unlimited Challenges: Participate in fun, planet-friendly fitness challenges.

  • Rewards: Earn perks for your efforts and commitment.

  • Customized Packages: Get tailored session packages designed to fit your goals and schedule —with exclusive savings!

Learn more and become a greengainer today—now that you know what it means!
