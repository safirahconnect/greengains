  • Workshops

    Our workshops are 90-minute hands-on, interactive sessions designed to inspire and educate. Each workshop focuses on practical skills for sustainable living, from reducing waste to adopting eco-friendly habits. Led by experts, these sessions include activities, discussions, and actionable tips you can apply in your daily life. All workshops are recorded and later uploaded to YouTube and TikTok to spread awareness about our community and mission.

    As a bonus, GreenGainers members get early and FREE access to ticket sales.

  • Outdoor Training

    Our weekend outdoor training sessions are 60-minute energizing workouts designed to connect you with nature while boosting your fitness. Held in local parks or green spaces, these sessions focus on functional exercises, group activities, and eco-friendly practices. It’s a perfect way to stay active, enjoy the outdoors, and meet like-minded GreenGainers.

  • Retreats

    Our retreats are immersive weekend experiences designed to help you reconnect with nature, recharge your energy, and deepen your commitment to sustainability. Set in serene, eco-friendly locations, these retreats include workshops, outdoor activities, and mindful practices. Held once a month, they offer a chance to unplug, learn, and grow alongside a supportive community of GreenGainers.

    As a bonus, GreenGainers members get early and FREE access to ticket sales.

  • Challenges & Rewards

    Our challenges are designed to inspire action and foster sustainable habits. From reducing plastic waste to lowering your carbon footprint, each challenge encourages you to make a positive impact on the planet. Complete the tasks, track your progress, and earn eco-friendly rewards like reusable water bottles, tote bags, and more!

  • Customized Packages

    Looking for a tailored experience? Our Customized Packages are designed to meet your unique needs and goals. Whether you’re planning a team-building retreat, a personalized fitness program, or a sustainability workshop, we’ll work with you to create a package that fits your vision.
